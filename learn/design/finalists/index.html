Lecture 1 · shortlist

Six designs, one slice of the lecture

Your shortlist: the three round-1 pages plus Living Map, On-Call and Episodes from round 3. All six show "Scaling the database" from Lecture 1. Open each and use it.

The first three are reading pages; the last three are interactive. Episodes is kept at the lowest priority.

01

Spec Sheet

Round 1 · page

A precise engineering datasheet: one reading column, margin notes beside the text, hairline diagrams on a dot grid, one orange for focus.

How to try it

  1. Scroll and read top to bottom
  2. Step through the replication figure with its buttons
  3. Open the 'pause' prompt to reveal the answer
Open Spec Sheet
02

Whiteboard Notebook

Round 1 · page

An engineer's notebook honouring the class whiteboard: serif text, hand-feel diagrams on graph paper, three marker colours with fixed meanings.

How to try it

  1. Scroll and read
  2. Step through the replication-lag swim lane
  3. Switch to dark with the 'Blackboard' button
Open Whiteboard Notebook
03

Explorable Essay

Round 1 · page

A long-form essay where a figure stays on screen and changes as you scroll; each part of the system owns one colour in text and figures.

How to try it

  1. Scroll slowly through the replication section to drive the figure
  2. Use the stepper on smaller screens
  3. Toggle motion and dark mode at the top
Open Explorable Essay
04

Living Map

Round 3 · interactive

Medium's architecture fills the screen. A 22-step tour changes it from one box to sharded databases, one sentence per step. Click any part for its card.

How to try it

  1. ← → or the round buttons; drag the bottom bar to jump chapters
  2. Click the small + on any part to open its card
  3. Try the sliders inside the cards
Open Living Map
05

On-Call at Medium

Round 3 · interactive

You are Medium's on-call engineer. Five incidents from the 09:00 newsletter spike to sharding: pick a move, watch the system react, read the debrief.

How to try it

  1. Pick a, b or c; wrong moves play out too, then retry
  2. Open the debrief cards after each outcome
  3. Tabs at the top jump between incidents
Open On-Call at Medium
06

Episodes

Round 3 · illustrated lowest priority

An illustrated episode, 'Too Many Readers': Postgres, the API servers, the load balancer, replicas and shards are characters. Every panel flips to an engineer view.

How to try it

  1. Click or press Enter on a panel to flip it
  2. 'Engineer view on every panel' flips them all
  3. Ends with the real numbers and three recall questions
Open Episodes
